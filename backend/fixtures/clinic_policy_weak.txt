Northbrook Family Clinic — Patient Privacy Policy
Last updated: March 2024

1. Introduction
Northbrook Family Clinic respects the privacy of our patients. We collect health
information in order to provide medical care.

2. How we use your information
We use your information to provide treatment, run our business, and contact you
about appointments. We may share information with vendors who help us operate
the clinic. We have a contract with our billing provider, Acme Billing.

3. Security
We take reasonable measures to keep your information secure. Our staff is
trained to handle records carefully.

4. Your rights
If you have questions, please contact the front desk.

5. Breaches
In the unlikely event of a breach, we will notify affected patients as soon as
practicable.
