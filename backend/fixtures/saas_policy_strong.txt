Helios Analytics — Privacy and Security Policy
Version 4.2 — Reviewed annually (Jan 2024)

1. Scope and System Description
Helios Analytics provides a hosted analytics platform. This policy covers our
production cardholder data environment (CDE), our customer-facing services, and
all subprocessors. Service boundaries and data flows are documented in our
Trust Center.

2. Lawful basis for processing (GDPR Art. 6)
We process customer personal data on the legal basis of (a) the contract with
the data controller and (b) our legitimate interest in operating the platform
securely. For marketing, we rely on consent, which can be withdrawn at any time
via the unsubscribe link in any email.

3. Data subject rights (GDPR Art. 15-20)
Data subjects may exercise their right to access, right to rectification, right
to erasure (right to be forgotten), right to data portability, and right to
object. Requests are answered within 30 days. Contact our Data Protection
Officer at dpo@helios.example.

4. International transfers
Transfers outside the EEA are governed by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses
(SCCs, 2021/914), supplemented by encryption in transit and at rest.

5. Retention
Customer logs are retained for 13 months and then purged. Account data is
retained for the duration of the subscription and for 90 days after termination.
A retention schedule is maintained as part of our Record of Processing
Activities (ROPA, GDPR Art. 30).

6. Breach notification
We notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of a
personal data breach (GDPR Art. 33). Affected individuals are notified without
unreasonable delay and no later than 60 days after discovery (HIPAA 45 CFR
164.404 for any covered PHI).

7. HIPAA — Covered services
For customers in the U.S. healthcare sector, Helios executes a Business
Associate Agreement (BAA) before any PHI is transmitted. Our Notice of Privacy
Practices is available on the Trust Center. We follow the minimum necessary
standard for PHI access. Patients have the right to access their health
information within 30 days of request.

8. Encryption
All cardholder data (PAN) and PHI is rendered unreadable using AES-256 at rest
and TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS) in transit. Card numbers are tokenized before storage; the
PAN never touches application logs.

9. Network segmentation
The cardholder data environment is segmented from out-of-scope systems via
isolated VLANs and host-based firewalls.

10. Multi-factor authentication
MFA is required for all administrative access and all access into the CDE
(PCI-DSS Req. 8.4).

11. Patch management
Critical security patches are applied within 30 days of release. Other patches
follow a monthly cadence (PCI-DSS Req. 6.3.3).

12. Vulnerability scans and penetration testing
Internal and external vulnerability scans are performed quarterly by an
Approved Scanning Vendor (PCI-DSS Req. 11.3). External penetration tests are
conducted annually and after significant changes (Req. 11.4).

13. Audit logging
All CDE systems forward audit logs to a centralized SIEM. Logs are reviewed
daily by our security operations team (PCI-DSS Req. 10).

14. Risk assessment
We perform a formal risk assessment annually and after significant changes.
Findings feed our risk register (TSC CC3.1 / 45 CFR 164.308).

15. Workforce training
All employees complete security awareness training at onboarding and annually
thereafter (TSC CC1.4 / 45 CFR 164.308(a)(5)).

16. Incident response
We maintain a documented incident response plan (PCI-DSS Req. 12.10 / TSC
CC7.3) that is tested annually.

17. Vendor risk management
Subservice organizations undergo a security review prior to onboarding and
annually thereafter (TSC CC9.2).

18. Backups and recovery
Customer data is backed up daily, encrypted, and stored in a secondary region.
Recovery procedures are tested quarterly with documented RTO of 4 hours and
RPO of 1 hour (TSC A1.2).

19. Access control
Role-based access control (RBAC) follows the principle of least privilege
(TSC CC6.1).

20. Continuous monitoring
Security events are continuously monitored via our SIEM, with 24/7 on-call
coverage (TSC CC7.2).

21. Change management
Changes to production systems require peer review, automated testing, and
documented approval (TSC CC8.1).

22. Privacy by design and default
We implement data minimization, pseudonymization where feasible, and default
privacy-respecting configurations (GDPR Art. 25).

23. Officers
Our Privacy Officer and Security Officer can be reached at
privacy@helios.example and security@helios.example respectively.

24. Children's data
The Helios platform is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly
collect data from minors without parental consent.

25. Information security policy review
This policy is reviewed annually and updated as required (PCI-DSS Req. 12.1.1).
